1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains what data is processed when you use the mobile game “Extreme Mad Micro Challenges” (the “Game”), available on Google Play and the App Store, and what rights you have regarding that data.

The data controller is the developer of the Game, X Labs Games (“we”, “us”). For any privacy-related question, contact us at xlabsgames@gmail.com.

The Game does not require an account. You can start playing without providing a name, email address, or phone number.

2. Data we process

2.1. Data you provide

If you contact support by email, we receive your email address and the content of your message. This data is used only to respond to your request.

2.2. Data collected automatically

When you launch and use the Game, advertising and technical services (see Section 3) may automatically receive:

  • the advertising identifier of your device (Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS);
  • your IP address and the approximate country or region derived from it;
  • device model and manufacturer, operating system version, system language;
  • type and speed of your network connection;
  • advertising events such as impressions, clicks, and dismissals;
  • technical crash and error logs needed to keep the Game stable.

2.3. Data that stays on your device

Game progress, settings, in-game items you have purchased, and local high scores are stored only on your device and are not transmitted to us. Uninstalling the Game removes this data.

2.4. What we do not collect

  • your name, surname, home address, or phone number;
  • precise (GPS) location;
  • contacts, photos, files, or calendar entries;
  • the content of your messages and conversations;
  • payment card details — purchases are processed by Google or Apple.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

The Game is free and supported by advertising. We use Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK), a service provided by Google LLC, to display ads.

AdMob may use your advertising identifier and technical data to select ads, limit ad frequency, prevent fraud, and measure advertising performance. Google’s handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and on the page “How Google uses data when you use our partners’ sites or apps”.

3.1. Consent to personalized advertising

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, consent for personalized advertising is requested through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) before such ads are shown. You can decline or change your choice at any time in the Game’s settings.

On iOS, access to the advertising identifier is granted only if you allow tracking via App Tracking Transparency. If you decline, you will see non-personalized ads.

3.2. How to turn off personalization

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID.
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Ads will still appear, but they will be less relevant to you.

5. Legal bases (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

If you are located in these regions, we process data on the following bases:

  • consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — for personalized advertising and access to the advertising identifier;
  • legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — fraud prevention, security, and stable operation of the Game;
  • performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) — providing access to the Game;
  • legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR) — where required by law.

You may withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

6. Sharing data

We share data only to the extent necessary to operate the Game:

  • Google LLC (AdMob, Google Play services) — ad delivery and measurement;
  • Apple Inc. — for installation and purchases through the App Store;
  • competent public authorities — only upon a lawful request.

We do not share data with advertising networks unrelated to the ads shown in the Game, and we do not disclose it for any other purpose.

7. Retention and deletion

We do not keep personal data longer than necessary: support requests are deleted within 12 months after the request is closed. Retention periods for data processed by Google in connection with advertising services are set by Google’s policies.

Game progress is stored on your device and is removed when you uninstall the Game. To delete data linked to your advertising identifier, reset or delete it in your device settings (see Section 3.2).

8. Security

Data transferred between the Game and Google services is protected by encryption (HTTPS/TLS). Access to support requests is restricted. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute protection against all threats.

9. Children

The Game is not intended for children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA and the UK), and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If the Game is designated as child-directed in an app store, ads are served without personalization and without the advertising identifier, in line with COPPA and Google Play and App Store policies.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided us with personal data, email xlabsgames@gmail.com and we will delete it.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on your region, you have the right to:

  • obtain information about the data we process and a copy of it;
  • request correction or deletion of your data;
  • restrict or object to processing;
  • withdraw consent to personalized advertising;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in the EEA, your local data protection regulator).

California residents have the right under the CCPA/CPRA to know which categories of data are collected, to request deletion, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of data. We do not sell personal data.

To exercise any of these rights, email xlabsgames@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days and may ask for details to verify that the request comes from you.

11. International transfers

Google services may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Such transfers rely on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses and other lawful mechanisms described in Google’s policy.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Policy, for example when adding new features or when legal requirements change. The current version is always available on this page, and the effective date is shown at the top. We will notify you of material changes in the Game or in the release notes.
